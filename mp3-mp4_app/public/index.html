MP4 to MP3 Converter
A free tool from Your AV Department
Files never leave this computer

Turn your session recordings into MP3s. Free.

Drop in your MP4 recordings and get an MP3 of each one. Nothing uploads. Your files never leave your computer.

Use it in your browser

No account. No email. No cost.

A whole conference at once

Drag in as many recordings as you have. Each one becomes its own MP3 with the same file name.

Private by design

The conversion runs on your computer. There is no upload, so speaker and member content stays with you.

Works offline once installed

Install it from this page in Chrome or Edge on Windows or Mac. It opens like any other app, no internet needed.

What planners use it for

  • Posting session audio for members who could not attend
  • Sending recordings out for transcripts or captions
  • Turning a large video file into audio that is a fraction of the size

Handles recordings up to about 2 GB each and saves standard 192 kbps MP3 files.
